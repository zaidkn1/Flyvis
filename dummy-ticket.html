Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Dummy Ticket?
A dummy ticket is a genuine, confirmed flight reservation with a real verifiable PNR code issued through airline reservation systems without purchasing the actual full-priced flight ticket. It is accepted by embassies and consulates worldwide as proof of intended onward travel.
Who uses Dummy Tickets?
Dummy tickets are commonly used by:
  • Visa applicants needing mandatory proof of return or onward travel for Schengen, UK, US, Canada, etc.
  • Passport renewal applicants whose local authority requests proof of upcoming travel.
  • Travelers at immigration checkpoints and border crossings requiring onward ticket confirmation.
  • Visa extension & status-change applicants needing temporary itinerary verification.
Will embassies accept this dummy ticket?
Yes, 100%. Embassies and VFS/TLS centres require a flight itinerary / reservation, not a paid non-refundable ticket. The PNR code we provide is live and verifiable directly on the airline's official website (e.g. Emirates, Qatar Airways, Lufthansa).
How long is the dummy ticket valid?
Dummy tickets are typically valid for 1 to 7 days, which is more than enough time for visa appointment submissions and initial consular checks.
Can I get a dummy hotel booking too?
Yes! Simply select the Hotel or Flight + Hotel tab above. We provide confirmed hotel reservations formatted specifically to satisfy embassy accommodation requirements.
