Photo Specifications Standard

All photo uploads must comply with ICAO standard biometric specifications:

  • 35mm x 45mm (80% face coverage)
  • Plain pure white background without shadows
  • Neutral facial expression, direct camera gaze
  • High resolution JPEG/PNG under 5MB

Passport Validity Rule

Embassies require a minimum passport validity of at least 6 months from the scheduled departure date, with at least 2 blank pages.
