Frequently Asked Questions

Is a dummy ticket the same as a real ticket?
No. A dummy ticket is a confirmed flight reservation with a real verifiable PNR — not a purchased ticket. It shows on the airline system long enough for visa application purposes.
Will embassies accept a dummy ticket?
Yes, embassies worldwide accept genuine dummy tickets. The PNR is verifiable directly on the airline website which satisfies embassy requirements for proof of onward travel.
How long is the dummy ticket valid?
Dummy tickets are typically valid for 1 to 7 days which is more than enough time for most visa interviews and submission processes.
How quickly will I receive my dummy ticket?
We deliver within 60 minutes of confirming your order on WhatsApp. For urgent cases we often deliver even faster — just mention it in your enquiry.
Can I get a dummy hotel booking too?
Yes! Select the Hotel or Flight + Hotel tab above. We provide dummy hotel reservations for visa applications that require proof of accommodation.
